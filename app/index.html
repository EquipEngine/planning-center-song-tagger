Connect to Planning Center

The tagger needs a Personal Access Token so it can read your songs and update their score tags. A token is two codes: a Client ID and a Secret.

Connecting needs the HV Song Tagger app. Planning Center doesn't let web pages use a key, so in a browser this page can only run the practice. Try it with the sample songs, then use the app for your real songs. About the app
  1. Open the Planning Center developer page and sign in.
  2. Under Personal Access Tokens, create a new one. Name it something like “Song tagging.”
  3. Copy the two codes into the boxes below. The Secret is shown only once, so keep that page open until you've connected.

Set up your score tags

You're connected, but Planning Center doesn't have a 1–5 score tag group yet. You only do this once, and it takes about two minutes.

1 Open the song tag settings

  1. In Planning Center, open Services, then Songs.
  2. Click Actions, then Edit Tags.
  3. Stay on the Song tab.

2 Add the score group and its tags

  1. Click Add Tag Group.
  2. Name it Horizontal Vertical Scale. Leave Allow multiple selections off, then click Save.
  3. In the new group, add these five tags. Each one must start with its number:
    • 1 - Horizontal
    • 2 - Mostly Horizontal
    • 3 - Blend
    • 4 - Mostly Vertical
    • 5 - Vertical

        What's next: using your scores

        Your scores are ordinary song tags, so they work everywhere Planning Center uses tags.

        Plan a service with them

        1. In a plan, add a song. The Select Song panel opens.
        2. Click Filter, then the Tags tab.
        3. Under your score group, tick the scores you want. For an opening of declaration, try 1 and 2; for a time of response, 4 and 5.
        4. The list narrows to matching songs. Pick one.

        Filter your song library

        1. Open Songs and click Filter.
        2. Choose the Tags tab, then pick a score under your score group.

        Handy for seeing how your most-used songs lean, or finding a vertical song for a season of prayer.

        See or change a song's score

        Open any song. Its score is listed under Tags, in your score group. You can change it right there, and the tagger will see the new score next time.
