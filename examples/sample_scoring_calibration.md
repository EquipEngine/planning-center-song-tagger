# Scoring calibration notes (example)

Claude keeps a file like this in the tool's folder, named `scoring_calibration.md`.
It records the calls your church has made, so every future scoring session
applies them the same way. Everything below is made up for illustration.

## Calibration batch, 2026-01-15

Ten songs were scored by our worship leader and by Claude. We agreed on 7
and talked through the other 3.

## House rules (how we apply the rubric)

1. **Descriptions sung to Jesus count as addressed.** If a song lists who
   Christ is but frames it as speaking to Him ("You are..."), treat it as
   vertical even when it's mostly description.
2. **Self-exhortation is horizontal.** Songs where the singer tells their own
   soul to praise stay at 1–2, even when the chorus feels worshipful.
3. **A single prayer line in a creed-style hymn doesn't move it off 1.**
   It takes a full section, such as a bridge or a chorus, to move it to 2.
